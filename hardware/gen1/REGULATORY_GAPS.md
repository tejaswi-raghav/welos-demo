# Regulatory and commercialization gaps

Status: design/specification only, 27 November 2026. No physical prototype has been assembled, energized or tested. Independent engineering review is required before procurement release and assembly.


This repository delivers a software interface and engineering plan. It establishes no product certification, lab safety approval, utility approval, building consent, licensed installer relationship or physical operating record.

## Before laboratory operation

Resolve actual board/module variants and datasheets, signed schematic/pin map, source/load protection, certified SELV supplies, pump/valve ratings, independent interlock design, water containment, calibration access and local lab safety authorization. Quote-required BOM lines and lead times remain open. The current reference firmware is not a verified Gen1 hardware build.

## Before a UAE field pilot

Dubai: use current DEWA enrolled electrical and DRRG EPC/consultant; confirm roof rights/structural assessment, equipment eligibility, capacity limits, design/NOC, inspection, agreement, bidirectional meter and commissioning. D33 is a distinct company captive-PV program. Neither is startup grant funding. Abu Dhabi: obtain a written current DoE/TAQA position because the self-supply licensing guide identifies small-scale residential/net-metering suspension while older netting regulations remain online. Sharjah: obtain SEWA project/export conditions; assume zero export credit until verified. Do not generalize DEWA rules to other emirates.

## Before an India field pilot

Identify actual state, DISCOM, tariff/category, sanctioned load and current technical process. Verify state metering rules, CEA safety/connectivity requirements, equipment conformity, installer authorization, roof/structural/fire permission, earthing/protection and commissioning documentation through a qualified EPC. PM Surya Ghar eligibility and DCR/ALMM/vendor requirements belong to the eligible residential customer and current scheme process. A monitoring retrofit alone is not assumed subsidy-eligible. No national export-credit rule applies uniformly to the six researched cities.

## Product and operating gaps

Assess applicable radio/telecom, EMC, electrical safety, environmental ingress/temperature, product liability, warranty, data privacy, cybersecurity and software access controls for each market. Exact standards and conformity pathway require competent test laboratory/legal/EPC review of the final product and use case. A module's FCC/CE or box IP marking cannot certify the assembled system. No drinking-water claims.

Official research: business/data/sources.json. No regulatory approvals or government applications have been submitted.
