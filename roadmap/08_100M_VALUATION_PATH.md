# US$100M valuation sensitivity

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Illustrative valuations, with EV = equity target + debt − excess cash. At zero net debt, these are stand-alone lenses and should not be added together.

| Lens | Assumption | Required basis for USD 100M |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware/service revenue | 1.5× revenue | USD 66.7M annual revenue |
| Software | 6× ARR | USD 16.7M ARR. At USD 600 ARR per account, about 27,778 accounts |
| EBITDA | 10× EBITDA | USD 10M EBITDA. At 20% margin, USD 50M revenue |
| Strategic acquisition | 20% illustrative uplift over standalone EV | USD 83.3M standalone EV |
| Downside | No recurring adoption and weak contribution | Preserve cash and reassess; no valuation supported by this model |

At a USD 2M pre-money and USD 500k round, pre-round holders retain 80%. Two equivalent rounds leave 64%, before options or other instruments. Change growth, attach, margins, working capital and unit costs in the founder app. Funding need is peak negative projected cash plus the agreed buffer, after excluding uncommitted financing.

A credible round requires signed pilots, customer retention, measured contribution after installs and support, enforceable data rights and a reproducible pipeline. Compare [SolarEdge results](https://investors.solaredge.com/news-releases/news-release-details/solaredge-announces-third-quarter-2026-financial-results), [Enphase filings](https://investor.enphase.com/) and [ABB's Sensorfact announcement](https://new.abb.com/news/detail/122814/abb-to-acquire-sensorfact-expanding-its-digital-energy-management-offering). Their product mixes, geographies and scale differ. The illustrative multiples above are independent scenario inputs.
