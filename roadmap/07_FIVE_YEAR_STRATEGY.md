# Five-year strategy

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Each gate needs positive evidence. If the deliverable is absent or the cost or permission gate fails, apply the fallback and repeat the decision review before spend or expansion.

| Timing | Owner | Cost ceiling | Dependencies | Success metric | Evidence for next decision | Failure fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 2 | Founder and product lead | Approved from demand | Repeatable first cohort | Support two documented inverter interfaces and consented multi-site trials | Integration tests and paid adoption | Partner-specific export adapter |
| Year 3 | Operations lead | Finance against orders | Repeatable QA and field support | Qualified second market pilot and SLA delivery | Local approvals, field support logs | Remain India-only |
| Years 4–5 | Founder and board | Capital based on cash model | Paid retention and audited economics | Repeatable enterprise sales and independent valuation evidence | Cohort reporting, margin and financial records | License workflow through EPCs, avoid manufacturing |
