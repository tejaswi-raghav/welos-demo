# Product and market priorities

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Scores are founder hypotheses from 1–5, with 5 favorable. Weights sum to 100. Score = weighted points ÷ 5. Replace each value after interviews.

| Direction | Feasibility 20 | Capital 15 | Demand 15 | Deployment 10 | Defensibility 10 | Recurring 15 | Margin 10 | Scale 5 | /100 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Battery-free monitoring and maintenance | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 73 |
| Integrated sun, wind, water | 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 38 |
| Hardware-agnostic energy software | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 78 |

Use monitoring as the first service experiment and hardware-agnostic software as the intended scale model. The latter scores highest but depends on data access and a workflow buyers pay for. The bench reduces measurement risk while the EPC discovery track validates permissioned integrations. Defer blended rooftop hardware until demand and field economics justify it.

Economic problem: service teams can lose truck rolls, time and PV production when relevant evidence is split between inverter portals, field notes and maintenance schedules. Quantify incident frequency, labor time, attributable kWh and time-to-resolution from real partner records. Do not infer a billion-dollar addressable market. Build a bottom-up reachable market from reachable EPCs × compatible sites × attach rate × annual paid monitoring fee. Compare with a rival's existing workflow as the control.
