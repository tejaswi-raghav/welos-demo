# Thirty interviews and three pilots

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Interview 12 Hyderabad EPC/service leads, 8 owners with 3–10 kW, 5 Indian C&I operators and 5 Dubai maintenance providers. Recruit via existing university and contractor networks with permission. Do not solicit confidential credentials. Ask for a recent underperformance incident, days to detection, avoided/actual visits and bills, cleaning frequency, tools, vendor access, spending authority, purchasing limit, objections and paid pilot threshold. Request anonymized artifacts where consent permits.

Evidence score: one point each for a dated incident, measured financial or time loss, approved data pathway, budget owner, and willingness to confirm paid scope. Score 4–5 with data and budget owner as qualified. Polite interest is recorded separately. Record reasons for declining.

Funnel: prospect → interview → qualified → scoped → authorized → active → paid/closed. Pilot proposal: approvals, read-only access, 6 weeks, named EPC and buyer, incumbent workflow baseline, comparison scorecard, actual cost and paid acceptance, revocable access, shutdown and data return. Ask for 3 scoped pilots only after engineering and interview gates.
