# Grant preparation pack

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Prepare entity/incorporation and ownership records if required, identity and residency evidence only in official private channels, IP disclosure with consent, aims, technology readiness, reviewed BOM, staged budget, test milestones, PI/reviewer commitment, prior-support disclosure, contingency, letters of interest, data governance and exit criteria.
