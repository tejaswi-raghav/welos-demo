# Funding shortlist and eligibility gaps

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

1. Confirm bank receipt, permissible spend, reporting terms and expiry of any university support. Treat possible BITS support as unconfirmed.
2. Prioritize approved lab time and reference instruments. Negotiate scoped customer-funded pilots when evidence and engineering release are sufficient.
3. Screen PRAYAS 2.0 only against a specific centre call. Programme maximum INR 20 lakh at a PC or INR 40 lakh at an APC is conditional, not an award. Confirm individual/entity status, citizenship requirements, prior grants, IP and TRL with current guidelines. [DST programme](https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/prayas2-0/)
4. MBRIF Accelerator offers support. The Guarantee Scheme supports lender debt and requires separate underwriting. [Official MBRIF FAQ](https://mbrif.ae/faq-2/)
5. Hub71 Access has equity-linked support terms. Verify cohort dates and Abu Dhabi obligations. Defer equity until partner evidence justifies dilution. [Hub71 Access](https://www.hub71.com/program/access-programme)
6. SISFS listed 31 May 2026 as its deadline. Keep it closed unless a subsequent official reopening is verified. [Official SISFS portal](https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/)

The [pipeline](FUNDING_PIPELINE.csv) preserves dated repository records and links. Confirm incubator/state cohorts, founder nationality, incorporation, sector, residency, prior funding and accepted costs. Customer subsidy cannot be booked into company cash. No award or eligibility is assumed.
