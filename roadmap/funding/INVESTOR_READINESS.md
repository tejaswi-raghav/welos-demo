# Investor readiness gates

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Angel or pre-seed review follows traceable hardware validation, paid or signed pilot evidence, actual contribution and runway reconciled to cash, supported data rights, owner commitments and a use-of-funds model with a stop trigger. Institutional capital follows repeatable cohorts, contracted channels and defensible retention. In-kind lab access and paid pilots precede debt and equity where feasible. Keep a private evidence room with records and a permission log.
