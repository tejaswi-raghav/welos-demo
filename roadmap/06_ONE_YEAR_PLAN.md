# One-year decision gates

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Each gate needs positive evidence. If the deliverable is absent or the cost or permission gate fails, apply the fallback and repeat the decision review before spend or expansion.

| Timing | Owner | Cost ceiling | Dependencies | Success metric | Evidence for next decision | Failure fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Months 4–6 | Founder and EPC | Separate approved pilot quote | Bench, consent, data agreement | Three timeboxed diagnostic trials | Pre/post service time and paid acceptance evidence | Run paid manual audit with one EPC |
| Months 7–9 | Founder | Stage from real receipts | Paid repeat usage | Positive contribution after CAC and field support | Invoices, labor logs, cohort counts | Simplify offer or sell partner workflow only |
| Months 10–12 | Founder and installer partner | From validated quote | Repeatable service | One signed distribution arrangement and retained subscription cohort | Signed commercial terms and measured renewals | Stay in one city and cap spend |
