# Provisional bench acceptance criteria

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Agree numeric bands with the reviewer before tests. Candidate gates: absolute power error within max(2 W, 3% of reference) across tested points after accounting for reference uncertainty, valid payload timestamp and provenance, 98% packet completeness during a supervised hour, and detection of 9 of 10 replayed data-loss events with at most one false alarm in 10 labeled controls. Record failures and rerun only after a causal correction. Evidence is raw logs, calibrated-reference proof, uncertainty calculation and independent sign-off. These gates do not establish performance outside the test conditions.
