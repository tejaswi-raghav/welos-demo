# Independent safety review

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Require review of cold-corrected PV open-circuit voltage, short circuit current, conductor and fuse ratings, load thermal duty, guarding, emergency isolation, earthed equipment, instrumentation isolation and the safe test plan. Check the bench environment, operators, labeling and emergency response before energization. Keep storage, water loop, rooftop work and grid tie outside this design. Stop on overheating, unexplained readings, damaged insulation or loss of supervision. Record every approval and operator. The documented [electrical controls](../../hardware/gen1/ELECTRICAL_SAFETY.md) are design inputs and require independent sign-off.
