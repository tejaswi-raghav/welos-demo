# Test and calibration sequence

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Pair the primary voltage/current readings with traceable references at five approved load points and repeat three times. Log IDs, calibration dates, temperature, irradiance, serial/time, raw measurements, uncertainty and rejected runs. Characterize drift, a disconnected data source, stale timestamps and inconsistent power without performing an unsafe electrical fault. Repeat a baseline on a second day. Keep an independent evidence log and preserve raw data separate from software simulation. Follow [the existing test plan](../../hardware/gen1/TEST_PLAN.md) and [telemetry contract](../../hardware/gen1/TELEMETRY_CONTRACT.md).
