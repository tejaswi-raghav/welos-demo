# Supervised dry bench build sequence

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

| Task | Responsible | Tools / spend | Dependency | Output and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm circuit and experiment boundary | Qualified engineer and founder | Design review allowance | Bench facility and vendor datasheets | Signed rating, protection, isolation and grounding decisions |
| Compare and receive approved parts | Procurement lead | Staged within approved landed BOM | Cash and engineer release | Part IDs, receipts and matching ratings |
| Inspect dry mechanical arrangement | Lab technician | Frame, guards and inspection checklist | Approved layout | Guarding, strain relief, ventilation and accessible shutoff verified |
| Bring up under supervision | Lab supervisor | Rated approved bench sources and reference meters | Inspection and authorization | Recorded reference readings and observed protection checks |
| Validate data and controlled fault cases | Firmware owner and technician | Timestamped logs and approved reference sources | Reference calibration | Read-only versioned snapshots and labeled raw data |
| Review release | Independent reviewer | Test/evidence register | Completed checklist | Signed result, limitations and decision on next experiment |

Use [the existing assembly guide](../../hardware/gen1/ASSEMBLY_GUIDE.md) as reference. The supervisor defines the physical sequence, isolation checks and permitted test conditions for the approved configuration.
