# First sellable product decision

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

| Candidate positioning | Clarity / differentiation / accuracy / relevance / range (1–5) |
|---|---|
| Recover visibility across your solar portfolio | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 5 |
| Solar fault triage for EPC service teams | 5 / 4 / 5 / 5 / 4 |
| Monitoring that turns alarms into service jobs | 5 / 4 / 4 / 5 / 4 |
| One maintenance view for supported inverters | 5 / 3 / 5 / 4 / 5 |
| Evidence-led rooftop performance | 3 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 |

Start with **solar fault triage for EPC service teams**. A scoped pilot covers supported inverter export/API, missing-data detection, a documented incident queue, weekly evidence review and consented before/after comparison. Initial diagnostic work stays read-only. A qualified EPC owns installation, inspection and commissioning. Test indicative INR 15,000 pilot fee against an illustrative INR 10,000 delivery allowance, followed by INR 699/site/month with INR 200 delivery cost assumption. Set a written scope, response window and stop date. Profile labor and site travel before treating the difference as margin. Negotiate warranty and support explicitly; avoid performance guarantees.

Core is the diagnostic offer. Intelligence adds repeatable triage and portfolio workflows only after paid usage and interoperability. Hybrid waits for distinct field demand, authorization and quantified returns. For buyers, the feature to prove is cross-brand evidence and resolution ownership beyond the manufacturer's monitoring app.
