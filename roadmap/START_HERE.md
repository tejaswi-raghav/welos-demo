# welOS founder roadmap

Assessment: 27 November 2026.

Sell a scoped **solar fault-triage pilot to a Hyderabad EPC** that already services 3–10 kW systems. Start with read-only evidence from an existing inverter and a supervised battery-free measurement bench. Test whether cross-brand incident workflow saves service time or finds actionable faults missed by incumbent tools.

1. Follow [the first seven days](03_FIRST_7_DAYS.md). Start with a written fault hypothesis, support documentation and a lab review appointment request.
2. Freeze the [bench scope and spend gates](prototype/PROCUREMENT_PLAN.md). The dry plan is AED 7,548 including contingency. Actual supplier offers, unrestricted cash and engineering approval remain required.
3. Use [the pilot and discovery scripts](sales/DISCOVERY_AND_PILOTS.md). Interview before quoting. Measure contractor time saved and verified energy recovery independently.
4. Keep editable cash, quotes and CRM records on your own device in the separate `welos-buz` workspace. Track evidence and dependencies using [the milestone dataset](data/milestones.json).
