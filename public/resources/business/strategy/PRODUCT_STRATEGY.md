# Product feasibility and readiness

| Variant | Customer/use | Readiness | Gate before sales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Gen1 | Battery-free monitoring retrofit and certified EPC integration | Software interface + lab specification | Physical measurement, interlocks, actual COGS and installer acceptance |
| Solar | Standard PV offer with monitoring | Existing planning estimator, no certified Welos installation | Site design/quote, equipment eligibility, utility/EPC commissioning |
| Hybrid | Storage/wind comparisons and future reliability use | Existing digital demo only | Separate protection/certification, resource data, replacement economics |
| Water | Non-potable sensing/rinse/possible recovery | Lab concept; existing digital modeling separate | Water safety, measured usefulness, plumbing approvals and service cost |
| Intelligence | Diagnostics, work orders and service history | Rule specification and validated snapshot adapter | Calibrated data, actionable-alert rate, paid retention, secure accounts |
| Enterprise | Multi-site portfolios | Discovery tool and future architecture | Tenant auth, device identity, privacy/security, fleet SLA and support economics |

Core Gen1 has no storage. Proposed field installations need approved grid-tied inverters. The lab rig measures a small module into a DC electronic load while auxiliary supplies power controls/water. Those demonstrations cannot validate grid export, rooftop structural safety or outage backup.

Existing product packages, aliases, localStorage builds, firmware and twin remain intact. The new wrapper uses the existing estimator rather than adding a competing bill model. Legacy packages are normalized only when explicitly passed to the new reader; the commercial portal does not write existing storage keys.

Defensibility must be earned through interoperable device adapters, validated maintenance outcomes, consistent commissioning data and service processes. No patent, proprietary algorithm advantage or exclusive partnership is claimed. Proposed IP audit: contributor agreements, asset/software licenses, firmware ownership and freedom to operate before grant/investor representations.
