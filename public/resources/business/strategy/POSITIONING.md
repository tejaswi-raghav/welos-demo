# Positioning and business model

welOS proposes an intelligent rooftop infrastructure operating layer for owners who need evidence that their solar assets perform and that maintenance resolves problems. The initial commercial hypothesis is a standardized battery-free monitoring retrofit, installed and commissioned through a qualified EPC. It can accompany a separately quoted approved solar installation, but Welos should not carry installation liability or full rooftop inventory before operational capability exists.

## Customer problem to validate

Interview owners about actual generation visibility, unexplained underperformance, cleaning decisions, service response and warranty handover. Ask for twelve months of bills and inverter records with permission. Do not infer willingness to pay from enthusiasm about the 3D demonstration. Quantify avoidable energy loss and current maintenance spending before proposing savings.

## Revenue hypotheses

1. Monitoring hardware plus documented installation coordination, priced ex-tax with clear exclusions.
2. Optional monitoring subscription for actionable diagnostics and records.
3. Optional annual maintenance contract with measured delivery cost.
4. Later installer software licensing and enterprise portfolio management, after pilots establish repeatable workflows.

The editable model initially includes only the first three streams. No software-license, enterprise or referral revenue is invented to improve valuation. No customer financing offer exists. Any future financing must come from an authorized provider with independent underwriting.

Initial beachhead: Hyderabad owners of existing 3–10 kW PV through one qualified installer. Local founder access and customer demand are assumptions to test, not established relationships. Dubai follows after three safe, documented local pilots and repeatable support economics. Product aesthetics and premium positioning should support clear scope, measured performance and dependable handover, with access through retrofit modularity and optional services rather than fabricated credit plans.
