# Site-specific regulatory checklist

This is an approval worklist for the qualified EPC, not legal certification. Rules and tariffs must be rechecked at quote date. Preserve documentary evidence for the exact site/account.

1. Establish legal customer, property/roof rights, actual utility/licensee, sanctioned load, meter/category and permission for telemetry.
2. Obtain current utility technical feasibility/export terms, capacity limits and eligible-equipment lists. Use actual account and correct jurisdiction; Delhi NCR is not one licensee.
3. Commission site electrical/structural/fire/water review by qualified personnel. Review local product/radio/EMC requirements for the final assembled monitoring product.
4. Select licensed/enrolled EPC; agree who owns design submissions, utility liaison, inspection, metering, commissioning and warranties. No direct grid connection by this lab rig.
5. Resolve meter/interface isolation, inverter-vendor permissions, data privacy, cybersecurity and support ownership before monitoring integration.
6. For India residential CFA, confirm customer, portal, vendor, module/DCR/ALMM, commissioning and bank documentation. Do not promise subsidy receipt or reduce Welos revenue using a customer grant.
7. For Dubai, separate Shams Dubai and D33 eligibility/conditions. For Abu Dhabi, get current written DoE/TAQA confirmation before any netting/export offer. For Sharjah, use zero export-credit assumption unless SEWA confirms otherwise.
8. Retain approval drawings, connection agreement, test certificates, owner handover, device inventory, calibration records and accepted commercial scope.

Known source conflicts: Abu Dhabi older netting regulations vs self-supply suspension guide; Telangana older FAQ vs 2025 regulation; legacy PRAYAS 10 lakh page vs PRAYAS 2.0 call; differing Khalifa Fund FAQ/product age criteria. Resolve the specific current instrument with the authority before application/installation.
