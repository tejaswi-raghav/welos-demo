# India market-entry report

Research date: 27 November 2026. Market prioritization is a strategic inference. No customer demand, partner relationship or subsidy qualification is established.

Priority: Initial beachhead. Target: Existing 3–10 kW residential/campus PV owners via one EPC.

Approval: TGSPDCL/TGNPDCL by service area; Telangana 2025 regulation.

Constraint: Partner access and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

## Customer economics and sourcing

Use actual interval load and annual bills, solar yield, site constraints, maintenance needs and EPC quotations. Export assumptions require account-specific confirmation. Show equipment, labor, permits, indirect taxes, O&M and replacements separately. No countrywide payback claim is supported by the current evidence. The approved UAE product estimator remains a scenario tool; India needs a site tariff/DISCOM calculation before a solar proposal.

Start with one retrofit supplier/installer pathway, obtain three comparable quotes and assess data access before procurement. Source ordinary fabrication/plumbing locally where quality documentation is adequate. Verify imported metering/sensor provenance and calibration. Local assembly is an evaluation path, not a manufacturing capability already established. No subsidy is booked as Welos company revenue.

PM Surya Ghar is a customer-side residential program. Confirm current national module/vendor/DCR/ALMM requirements and state/DISCOM rules before publishing net customer cost. Banks may offer customer finance through their authorized channels; Welos has no lender arrangement. The six cities do not share a universal metering or state incentive policy.
