# Supplier and installer research

The real component list and dated public-price observations are in hardware/gen1/BOM.csv and business/data/prototype.json. India candidates include Visha World (ESP32-S3 listing), element14 India and local fabrication; UAE candidates include Microless (test module listing), RS UAE and local qualified fabricators. DFRobot and Adafruit manufacturer pages establish actual sensor/board specifications and public prices where captured. They do not establish local stock, a distribution contract or final landed cost.

Procurement holds: obtain formal quote, exact SKU/revision, stock/lead time, shipping location, tax/duty, substitution approval, provenance and return/warranty terms. Compare at least three offers for frame/protection/power supplies, without compromising certified supplies or measurement references. Budget ceilings include headroom; those are estimates, not supplier-accepted prices.

Installer sourcing starts with the actual utility enrollment list in Dubai and applicable utility/vendor processes in India. Select by jurisdiction, competence, approved equipment and service capability. No named contractor is a confirmed Welos partner.

Borrow/rent a calibrated reference pyranometer and DMM from a supervised lab. If access cannot be secured within budget, defer measurement claims rather than substitute lux readings. Fit the sensor's calibrated flow range to the actual pump loop. A quoted component is not accepted until its electrical/mechanical compatibility is reviewed.
