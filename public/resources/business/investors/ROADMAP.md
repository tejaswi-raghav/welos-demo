# Founder execution roadmap

Owners below are proposed workstreams for the two named founders; assign responsibilities explicitly at kickoff. No role or expertise is inferred from names.

| Horizon | Work | Measurable acceptance | Dependency / stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days1–30 | Scope, incorporation/IP facts, quotes, engineering review, prototype interfaces, customer discovery | Signed scope/safety review; >=3 supplier/EPC quotes; instrument access confirmed; cap incl contingency<=AED10k;20 owner and5 EPC interviews; named workstream owners | No procurement/energization without reviewer and unrestricted funds |
| Days31–90 | Assemble only with physical resources, calibrate, test interlocks/telemetry, prepare demo, draft pilot agreements, screen funding | Raw P01–P13 records;>=8h logging target>=95% coverage; all safety faults closed;3 qualified pilot intents; program eligibility evidence checked; no expired-window applications | Physical results unavailable until actually run; failed safety blocks public demo |
| Months4–12 | Certified/approved field pilot, willingness to pay, service operations and supplier repeatability |3 safely commissioned paid/clearly priced pilots;30-day data each; warranty/support ownership; positive contribution after CAC for repeated scope; paid monitoring usefulness/renewal recorded | One city only until installation quality and cash/service cost reconcile |
| Year2 | Repeatable partner operations and multi-site software evaluation |2 independently qualified installers;10 consecutive accepted handovers; documented unit-cost variance within15%; six-month paid renewal cohort; monthly cash plan funded | Do not add enterprise SLAs without tenant auth/security/support readiness |
| Year3 | Controlled next-city/country entry, manufacturing assessment | Written local approvals path,3 local quotes and positive pilot contribution; independent make/buy/quality analysis | No expansion based solely on TAM or subsidy headlines |
| Years4–5 | Recurring/service scale, diversified channels, investor readiness | Cohort retention/gross margin and audited revenue; no single partner dominating plan without risk treatment; cash funded through growth cycle; complete data/IP/security diligence | $100M target remains sensitivity; equity/debt decisions require actual results |

Weekly cadence: review safety issues, pipeline stages, spent/committed cash, data coverage, service incidents and next evidence. Monthly: compare forecast assumptions with actual costs/conversion/retention; revise pace and capital needs. Grant applications and partner outreach require the relevant founder to verify factual completeness and terms before submission.
