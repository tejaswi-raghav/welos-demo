# Ground-level demonstrator mechanical design

Status: design/specification only, 27 November 2026. No physical prototype has been assembled, energized or tested. Independent engineering review is required before procurement release and assembly.


Scope: portable supervised rig, not a roof canopy or occupied building structure. Budget assumes local fabrication; drawings and quotes remain procurement holds.

Use the selected flexible panel only with the supplier's approved support method, minimum bend radius and heat clearance. A rigid full-support frame is preferred for repeatable test geometry. No holes, adhesives or loads may violate module instructions. Put the irradiance reference in the same plane with documented leveling/tilt and no persistent frame shadows. Log geometry before comparisons.

Place the 20 L tank low, within a tray sized for its entire contents, with electronics and mains adapters outside that containment volume. At approximately 20 kg when full, water dominates tip-over risk. Verify actual mass, center of gravity and restraint under the intended demonstration surface and supervised handling conditions. Provide guards for hose fittings, hot electronic load surfaces and sharp frame edges. Retain clear hand access to E-stop and isolation.

The cleaning demonstrator is a fixed low-pressure rinse rail with manual supervised start. No autonomous rail/robot, moving rooftop carriage, high-pressure jet or rotating wind device is included. Secure rail and drain paths so spray cannot reach electronics or the audience. Test on a sacrificial surface before exposing the PV module. Confirm the module permits water cleaning and observe thermal-shock/chemical restrictions.

Acceptance: signed drawing revision, fastener/strain-relief checklist, stable full/empty-tank configuration, no spray outside containment, comfortable supervised stop access and measured electronic-load surface temperature below the engineer-approved limit. All dimensions and transport loads require the fabricator and reviewer to approve before fabrication.
