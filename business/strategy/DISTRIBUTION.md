# Original distribution model

Premium positioning should come from product consistency, transparent evidence and after-sales reliability. Accessible ownership should come from a small retrofit scope, optional paid services and a clear comparison with alternatives. Danube's recognizable positioning and repeatable distribution are strategic inspiration only. No affiliation, copied branding, installment plan or financing approval is claimed.

## Standard commercial process

Discovery call; permissioned bill/asset review; EPC site survey; itemized equipment/install/service quote; approval responsibilities; measured commissioning acceptance; operator handover; 30-day usefulness review; optional service renewal. Offer the same scope/checklist to each partner. Keep prospect, qualified, contracted, commissioned and renewed stages distinct in the pipeline.

Qualify partners for local licenses/enrollment, engineering competence, insurance/liability, compatible equipment, installation QA, service response, spares and data protection. Audit the first three installations together. Do not award a Welos certification until a defined training/test/audit scheme exists. Use the term qualified prospective installer meanwhile.

## Six usable conversation briefs

**Residential customer.** Request electricity bill, inverter model and roof rights. Offer assessment of monitoring gaps. Deliver an itemized retrofit quote with optional service and full system exclusions. Acceptance: stable data and an understandable handover, not a promised saving or backup supply.

**Property developer.** Request portfolio/site ownership, construction stage and facility manager. Propose one representative property and a repeatable specification. Deliver interface requirements, approvals matrix and handover checklist. Acceptance: a complete safe installation record and support ownership.

**EPC company.** Request license/enrollment evidence, installed inverter mix and O&M process. Propose a co-delivered monitoring pilot. EPC owns approved electrical work, Welos owns defined monitoring scope. Deliver device compatibility and commissioning tests. Negotiate warranty boundaries and referral/install fees openly.

**Commercial building owner.** Request daylight load, meter category, landlord/tenant consent and existing PV records. Evaluate on-site consumption first. Deliver a measured pilot baseline and monitoring scope. Do not underwrite Abu Dhabi/Sharjah export revenue without written current permission.

**University.** Request supervised workshop access and reference instruments. Propose a low-voltage research demonstrator with independent engineering review and consented data. Deliver test protocol and raw results. No unsafe public energized demonstration before review.

**Government demonstration sponsor.** Identify formal tender/program eligibility and the authorized entity. Submit a scoped engineering proposal only through the appropriate channel. Deliver evidence of safety, actual costs and measurable acceptance. Do not imply subsidy approval or government endorsement.

## Financing and service

Collect written terms only from appropriately authorized lenders when the customer requests finance. No Welos credit approval, APR or repayment plan is offered here. Plan spare inventory, response escalation and warranty reserve before accepting recurring service fees. Require actual installation and subscription contribution after CAC to be positive before increasing acquisition spend.
