# Capital requirements and funding milestones

The initial engineering budget is AED10k. The planned lab spend is AED8,556 including contingency, subject to actual quotes. Funding availability is founder-reported and unverified. No additional government award, customer deposit, equity or bank finance is booked.

The five-year model calculates incremental cash required under three scenarios before financing. Review the /investors model or FIVE_YEAR_MODEL.csv for current values; avoid copying stale forecasts into a pitch. Annual modeling misses intra-year cash troughs and financing fees. Prepare a monthly 18-month treasury plan before inventory commitments.

Capital release gates:

1. Engineering: actual quotes within cap, signed safety plan and instrument access. Spend only from confirmed unrestricted cash.
2. Prototype validation: raw results against test plan, faults fixed, proof of safe supervised operation. Eligibility-screen PRAYAS/appropriate support before applying.
3. Pilot readiness: licensed EPC and site agreement, approval pathway, insurance/warranty boundaries, support cost and paid acceptance criteria. Budget each pilot separately.
4. Commercial repeatability: three safely commissioned pilots and tested willingness to pay, contribution margin after CAC and evidence of paid monitoring usefulness.
5. Growth capital: reconcile forecast to actual cohorts and installer capacity; fund working capital and support before geographic expansion. Evaluate equity/debt with explicit repayment/dilution rather than assuming grants close a structural loss.

Review downside with opening cash zero, AR90 days, COGS/CAC +25%, volume halved and attach reduced. A negative case should change scope or pace; it is not solved by declaring a $100M valuation.
