# WELOS pilot proposal

Draft for a prospective owner/EPC/university. No agreement or transmission has occurred.

## Scope

Phase A: supervised low-voltage lab demonstrator, separate from building/utility wiring. Phase B, only after PhaseA and site approvals: three monitored existing-PV retrofits in one city via a qualified EPC. New PV/inverter/canopy, utility exports, battery backup, potable water, performance guarantees and financing are excluded unless covered by a separately reviewed contract.

## Responsibilities

Owner: rights to site/access/data, actual bills, approvals and named operator. EPC: licensed engineering/site work, utility submissions where needed, safety/structural review, equipment commissioning, as-built records. Welos: defined monitoring equipment/interface, data labeling, diagnostics, operator documentation and scoped support. Qualified reviewer: independent design/test review. All parties agree warranty/liability, insurance, access and change control before site work.

## Acceptance and measurement

Record14 days baseline where available and30 days monitored operation. Retain instrument/calibration/firmware/device details, expected sample counts, valid-data coverage, faults and intervention records. Target >=95% expected telemetry coverage, no unresolved safety-critical findings, validated sensor agreement per TEST_PLAN and documented owner handover. Compare energy under matched resource/load conditions rather than attributing all bill changes to software. Paid service willingness is measured by an actual accepted price/payment or renewal, not a survey alone.

## Commercial terms to negotiate

Provide an itemized quote after survey, including hardware, EPC installation, taxes, travel, approvals and optional service. No price in the company scenario model constitutes a binding project quote. Define payment milestones, acceptance, warranty, site damage responsibility, termination, data rights/privacy and response obligations in a reviewed agreement. Any customer subsidy belongs to the customer and remains subject to approval.

## Exit and decision

Success supports the next controlled rollout; insufficient data triggers a revised test, not a savings claim. Any unsafe condition stops operation and requires review. Return/remove equipment and data according to agreed terms. A public case study requires explicit owner consent and verified evidence. No actual site or partner is represented as contracted.
